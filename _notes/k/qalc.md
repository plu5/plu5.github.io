## Commandes
- 100 EUR to USD
- 0xFF to bin
- 1<<11 to hex
- 1<<11|0x400 to hex
- infinity to float
  + IEEE 754
- -infinity to float
- floatBits(3.14) to bin
- float(0100 0000 0100 1000 1111 0101 1100 0011)
- floatError(3.14)

## Rappels
- 2 chiffres hex = 1 byte (8 bits) (= octet)
- 1 chiffre hex = nibble/nybble
